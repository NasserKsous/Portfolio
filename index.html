My Projects

DirectX Engine

This is a graphics engine I have made using DirectX. The video shows off a boat moving with multiple cameras set up and waves.

This engine was made during my second year at university. For the graphics side, I made use of shaders to create lighting and to create the movement of the waves. To create the boat I used an OBJ loader and applied a texture onto the obj. This is a massive improvement from the OpenGL engine I made in my first year and I'm especially happy with my use of a shader file. Shaders were a nw concept going into my 2nd year and therefore I had to do a lot of outside research so that I could use them effectively in my new engine.

The physics side was created in the second half of my 2nd year. This involved simulating real world physics in the graphics engine that was made in the first half of the year. The physics implmented include gravity, bouyancy, torque and rotation.

This is a graphics and physics engine made in my second year at University.

FPS Rogue-Like Game

This is a first person shooter rogue-like game made in 8 weeks as part of a University project.

This is a group game made as part of the technical games production module at university. We had a group of 4 and my part was creating the AI enemies and the boss character. To do this I created a melee, ranged and boss behaviour trees and the perception sensors. These used tasks which were created using the c++ integration in UE4.

I created 5 different enemy types:
  • Melee enemy that is a similar to a zombie that deals damage when the hit collider on the hand hits the player.
  • Explosive melee that explodes a few seconds after hitting the player.
  • Ranged enemy that throws spears that will deal damage it they hit the player.
  • Spawner that will spawn melee enemies and put up a wall if the play gets too close.
  • Boss enemy with 6 different attacks (3 that are randomly choosen and then 3 different attacks after the boss gets below half health.

This was my first attempt at making a game using the c++ with UE4.
